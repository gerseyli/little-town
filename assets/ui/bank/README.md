# City Bank UI artwork

Four generated, transparent HD assets, converted to lossless WebP without resizing. Existing lobby and map are unchanged. All screen text, buttons, account values and house cards are live HTML; no text is baked into the monitor art. Dollar stack is a decorative game prop, not a representation of the transaction denomination/count. CSS clips it at the slot and moves it in/out; reduced-motion uses a short animation. Original generated PNGs remain in the generation workspace.

Native sizes: ATM 1122×1402; cash slot 2172×724; dollar stack 1774×887; mortgage computer 1496×1051.

## Generation prompts

### ATM

Use the ATM at the far LEFT of the supplied City Bank lobby as the visual design reference. Generate ONE standalone front-facing close-up of its upper body, an elegant creamy ivory and warm brushed-metal ATM with rich burgundy accents and subtle brass details, cozy semi-realistic whimsical storybook game art matching that bank exactly. Portrait 4:5 composition, isolated on transparent background, machine fills nearly all canvas. A VERY LARGE completely blank dark navy-blue rectangular monitor dominates the upper middle: left 10%, right 90%, top 16%, bottom 66% of canvas. The monitor must be plain and unlit enough for high-contrast live HTML controls to be added later, no baked-in controls or writing. A slim burgundy crown and simple tiny pig/coin emblem above the screen, no letters. Below the screen leave a clean creamy flat fascia area from about 70% to 94% of height to mount a separately drawn money slot later. No slot or cash on this image, no keypad competing with the monitor, no floor, no room, no human. Rounded tactile corners, realistic material detail, straight orthographic view, warm indoor lighting, coherent high-definition crisp edges. No text, no numerals, no watermarks. Keep display and lower fascia unornamented and unobstructed.

### Cash slot

Generate a single isolated close-up money dispenser/deposit slot for a warm ivory City Bank ATM, matching creamy enamel, warm brushed stainless metal, subtle antique brass bevel and a restrained burgundy accent. Perfectly straight-on front view; wide horizontal 5:1 shape. A crisp black horizontal slit across the middle with realistic depth, neat lower metal lip and rounded frame. Upper and lower edges clearly defined for a game cash animation. No banknotes, no hands, no text, no symbols, no LEDs with writing. Semi-realistic very detailed warm storybook game illustration, slightly whimsical tactile edges, warm indoor light, coherent geometry and sharp clean silhouette. True transparent outside the rectangular slot plate, slot fills canvas width with minimal empty margin. High definition.

### Cash

Generate ONE compact stack of US-dollar-style green banknotes as a transparent game prop, viewed from almost straight above with a little 3D thickness, long edges horizontal, about 2:1 width-to-height silhouette. The top bill has recognizable tasteful green engraved currency-style borders, a central oval portrait motif and clear dollar signs, understated denomination marks; make it a coherent illustrated dollar prop rather than fragile tiny text. Several aligned note edges visible beneath, slight natural curve, beautifully realistic paper texture and warm highlights, subtle storybook/cartoon softness matching a cozy high-detail City Bank game. Crisp silhouette designed to slide vertically in and out of a front-facing ATM slot. No flying loose bills, no coins, no hands, no bands, no scene, no extra separate stacks, no watermark. True transparent background, little to no outside shadow, stack centred and filling a wide landscape canvas, high definition.

### Mortgage computer

Create a high-definition illustrated BANK DESK COMPUTER MONITOR game UI frame. Match the supplied City Bank lobby: warm elegant ivory, burgundy, polished brass and walnut, realistic slightly whimsical storybook 3D cartoon rendering. One front-facing desktop monitor with a compact walnut desk ledge and sturdy ivory/brass base. Wide landscape overall image, approximately 16:11. Large perfectly rectangular BLANK dark navy display, at least 82% of image width and 70% of height, for later live HTML house catalog overlay. Very thin elegant ivory metal bezel, subtle brass trim, tiny pink pig coin emblem centered at bezel bottom. Front orthographic view, no perspective distortion. Transparent outside silhouette. No text, letters, labels, buttons, houses, people, mouse or keyboard. No reflections obscuring the screen. Sharp coherent edges, generous unobstructed screen. This is the mortgage service computer inside the reference bank.

The ATM and mortgage monitor used `assets/interiors/bank/background.png` as a style reference. Slot and dollar stack were generated independently with the prompts above.
