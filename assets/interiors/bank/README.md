# City Bank lobby

`background.png` is the original **Whimsical City Bank Department Lobby.png** supplied in Downloads.rar, also shown again by the user on 2026-11-09. It is used unchanged at its native **1672×941** resolution. The original filename and SHA-256 hash are recorded in `../source-manifest.json`.

The Town bank now uses this background through the shared entrance/interior system. The pharmacy portrait, greeting and shop are hidden. Five aligned interactive regions open the bank services. ATM and mortgages use generated illustrated close-up screens; account/loan/calendar state is saved separately from the spendable wallet. See `docs/BANKING.md` and `assets/ui/bank/README.md`. The lobby itself remains unchanged.
