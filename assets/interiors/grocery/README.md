# Grocery store artwork

New artwork generated for the Grocery store pilot. Native-resolution images are converted to lossless WebP without resizing or physical cropping. The three 4×2 sprite sheets are addressed with SVG viewBox cells, retaining all 24 illustrations separately in the interface. Captions, phrases, prices and basket controls are live HTML. Original generated PNGs remain in the generation workspace.

## Prompts

### background

Create a high-definition 16:9 interior background for a cozy small-town GROCERY STORE in an elegant realistic slightly whimsical storybook cartoon game style. Front-facing view from customer eye height. Warm cream and sage-green wooden shelves, walnut counter spanning lower third with an UNCLUTTERED central service area where a separate shop assistant portrait will be composited behind it. Background shelves contain beautiful carefully recognizable groceries: tins with pictures of beans, jars of honey/jam, cartons of milk/eggs, packets of pasta, paper bags of flour, tubs of yogurt; fresh apples, carrots, tomatoes and bananas in wooden produce crates on the left. On right tasteful fridge and bread display, cloth shopping bags and a small cash register. Daylight through left shop window, warm pendant lamps, small potted herbs, beautiful coherent decorative details. Wide uncluttered middle at center-left for shop assistant. No people anywhere. No medicine, hospital crosses, ATM, liquor or brands. No legible writing or lettering on any signs. Detailed HD artwork that holds up on a large computer screen; restrained coherent objects, no warped shapes, no visual artifacts.

### assistant

Generate ONE friendly male grocery shop assistant, about 40, warm medium-brown skin, kind expressive brown eyes, short soft curly dark hair with slight gray at temples, a small tidy mustache, natural welcoming smile. Sage-green grocer's apron over a cream rolled-sleeve shirt, subtle burgundy neckerchief, plain little brass name badge with no writing. Waist-up portrait facing the viewer squarely, empty hands relaxed folded at waist, anatomically correct five fingers, no groceries or props. Cozy semi-realistic whimsical storybook 3D cartoon style, highly detailed warm rendering, believable fabric/skin, friendly and approachable, matching Little Town pharmacy and bank artwork. Portrait 4:5, fills frame without cutting head or elbows. True transparent background, no scenery, no counter, no text, no extra states.
